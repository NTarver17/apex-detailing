Why Choose a Vinyl Wrap Over a Repaint?

Historically, changing the exterior color of an exotic sports car or luxury vehicle required a permanent, irreversible respray that dramatically degraded vehicle resale value and voided factory rust warranties.

Modern cast vinyl wrap films allow 100% complete visual transformation while preserving 100% of your vehicle’s original OEM factory paint underneath. When you are ready to sell the car or try a new aesthetic, the film is cleanly removed, revealing pristine factory paint in the exact condition it was in on day one.

Cast Vinyl vs Calendared Economy Vinyl

Apex exclusively installs Premium Cast Films (3M 2080, Avery Supreme Wrapping Film, Inozetek, and STEK Color PPF). Unlike cheap calendared films that shrink, crack, and pull back out of body recesses after six months in the Arizona heat, cast vinyl has zero memory tension and remains dimensionally stable for 5 to 7 years.

The Disassembly & Installation Standard

To achieve seamless, paint-like wrap installations with zero exposed seams or lift points:

  1. Meticulous De-Trim: Headlights, taillights, door handles, side mirrors, and emblems are safely removed using certified non-marring tools.
  2. Cleanroom Alcohol Prep: Every panel seam, rubber door jamb, and under-hood lip is cleaned of road grime and degreased with isopropyl alcohol.
  3. Deep Edge Tucking: Vinyl is heated and wrapped 1/2-inch around the backside of every panel, preventing edge peeling at highway speeds.
  4. Post-Heat Memory Reset: Every stretched contour is post-heated to 195°F - 215°F using infrared laser thermometers to break the vinyl's original memory, guaranteeing it never pulls back or bubbles.
