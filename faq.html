Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, comprehensive answers regarding automotive paint protection film, 9H ceramic coatings, paint correction, warranties, and aftercare maintenance.

Paint Protection Film (PPF)

How long does Paint Protection Film (PPF) last?

Our premium 8.5 mil films are rated for 10+ years of active durability. Every installation includes a comprehensive 10-year nationwide manufacturer warranty covering yellowing, hazing, cracking, bubbling, and delamination.

Will PPF damage my vehicle's factory paint if I ever remove it?

No. When installed over sound OEM factory paint, professional PPF utilizes specialized acrylic adhesives that can be safely removed by a professional with steam heat at any point without lifting clear coat or leaving sticky residue.

What is the difference between computer-cut kits and Apex custom wrapped edges?

Standard software templates leave an exposed 1/16" gap around every panel edge that collects dirt lines over time. At Apex, our technicians digitally extend every pattern to wrap 1/4" to 1/2" around the backside panel seams, rendering the film virtually undetectable.

Ceramic Coatings & Chemistry

Can ceramic coating protect my vehicle from rock chips?

No. Ceramic coatings measure only 2 to 3 microns thick and are engineered for chemical defense (UV rays, bird droppings, acid rain) and extreme water beading. To stop physical high-speed rock chips and road debris, Paint Protection Film (215 microns thick) is required.

Can you apply ceramic coating over Paint Protection Film?

Yes! This is our most popular "Hybrid Stack" package. Applying a dedicated ceramic coating over PPF gives you the rock chip protection of film paired with the high-contact-angle self-cleaning slickness and gloss of ceramic glass.

Paint Correction & Studio Standards

Does a brand new car from the dealership need paint correction?

Surprisingly, yes. During factory transport (rail cars, cargo ships, open trailers) and dealership prep washes, new vehicles accumulate rail dust, transport wrap adhesive residue, and micro-swirls. A single-stage or two-stage machine polish is essential to ensure the paint is flawless before locking it under film or ceramic.

How long do you need the car for a full wrap or coating?

- Full Body PPF: 3 to 5 business days.
- Track Pack PPF: 2 business days.
- Ceramic Coating & 2-Stage Correction: 2 business days.
- Color Change Vinyl Wrap: 5 to 7 business days.
