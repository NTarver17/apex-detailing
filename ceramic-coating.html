What is a Professional 9H Ceramic Coating?

A professional ceramic coating is an inorganic liquid polymer formulation primarily composed of high-purity silicon dioxide (SiO2) and titanium dioxide (TiO2) suspended in a volatile organic solvent carrier. When hand-applied to a properly prepped and polished automotive clear coat, the molecules seep into the microscopic pores of the lacquer.

As the solvent flashes and evaporates, the silane compounds cross-link with the clear coat molecules through a chemical curing process, creating an ultra-dense, semi-permanent quartz matrix measuring between 1.5 to 3.0 microns in thickness.

What Does "9H Hardness" Mean?

The "9H" rating refers to the standardized Wolf-Wilburn pencil hardness test (ASTM D3363), measuring resistance to surface scratching compared to graphite grades. Factory clear coat typically scores around 3H to 4H. A professional ceramic coating hardens the sacrificial surface to 9H, dramatically reducing wash marring and fine micro-swirls.

Ceramic vs Graphene Coatings: What Is the Difference?

Graphene coatings represent an evolutionary iteration of ceramic technology where reduced graphene oxide (rGO) nanosheets are blended into the siloxane matrix.

  • Thermal Dispersion: Graphene's honeycomb carbon lattice conducts and dissipates heat across the panel faster, reducing the surface temperature of dark colored cars in the Arizona sun.
  • Water Spot Reduction: The lower surface free energy reduces mineral deposit etching from hard tap water or sudden desert monsoons.
  • Anti-Static Properties: Graphene naturally repels airborne dust and brake dust particles more effectively than pure SiO2.

The Multi-Step Cleanroom Installation Protocol

A ceramic coating is only as good as the surface underneath it. At Apex, over 80% of our labor time is dedicated to decontamination and surface refinement:

1

Chemical & Mechanical Decon

Citrus foam pre-wash, iron deposit fallout dissolver, tar extraction, and synthetic clay bar treatment to remove all embedded brake dust and road contaminants.

2

Paint Correction & Leveling

Multi-stage machine compounding and ultra-fine jeweling polish under high-intensity inspection LEDs to eliminate 85% to 95%+ of all factory swirls and haze.

3

Solvent IPA Wipe Down

Full panel wipedown using pure Isopropyl Alcohol and panel preparation spray to strip every trace of polishing oils, ensuring 100% naked clear coat bonding.

4

Precision Hand Application & IR Cure

Applied in cross-hatch patterns, leveled with ultra-plush microfibers, and cured under shortwave infrared lamps to accelerate cross-linking to full 9H hardness.

How to Care for a Ceramic Coated Vehicle

While ceramic coatings make washing your car 5x faster, they are not completely maintenance-free. To preserve peak hydrophobicity:

  1. Never take the vehicle through automatic tunnel washes with rotating abrasive nylon brushes.
  2. Employ the two-bucket method with grit guards or a touchless foam cannon setup.
  3. Dry using a high-velocity heated air blower or a 1,200 GSM twisted-loop microfiber drying towel with a ceramic drying aid.
  4. Book an annual decontamination wash and ceramic booster inspection at our studio to clear clogged mineral pores.
