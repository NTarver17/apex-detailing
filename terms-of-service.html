Terms of Service & Warranty Agreement

Please review the terms and conditions governing vehicle restyling, paint protection film installation, ceramic coatings, and studio appointments at Apex Paint Protection LLC.

1. Scope of Services & Initial Vehicle Inspection

Prior to performing any paint correction, PPF installation, ceramic coating, or vinyl wrap, an authorized Apex technician will conduct a comprehensive multi-point intake inspection under clinical studio lighting. Existing paint defects, rock chips, prior touch-ups, repainted panels, clear coat failure, and body misalignment will be digitally photographed and noted on your intake agreement.

2. 10-Year Manufacturer Film Warranty Coverage

All premium paint protection films installed by Apex carry a 10-Year Transferable Manufacturer Warranty against:

  • Yellowing, discoloration, or hazing from UV exposure.
  • Cracking, crazing, or blistering of the polyurethane core.
  • Delamination or bubbling caused by adhesive failure.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by severe vehicle collisions, automated car wash brush damage, intentional abuse, acid chemical burns, or failure to follow prescribed aftercare guidelines.

3. Post-Installation Curing & Client Aftercare

Paint Protection Film and Ceramic Coatings require an initial curing period. The client agrees to adhere strictly to the following requirements:

  • Do not wash, spray, or expose the vehicle to moisture for at least 7 calendar days following completion.
  • Tiny moisture pockets or hazy slip solution channels are a normal part of the TPU curing process and will naturally evaporate through the film's microscopic pores within 14 to 21 days in warm ambient temperatures.
  • A complimentary 14-day post-installation inspection is included with every full-body package to check edge seal adhesion.

4. Booking Deposits & Cancellations

Due to our dedicated multi-day cleanroom scheduling, a 20% deposit is required to reserve installation dates for full-body clear bra or color change wraps. Deposits are fully refundable or transferable with at least 72 hours advance notice prior to your scheduled drop-off date.

5. Limitation of Liability

Apex Paint Protection LLC maintains comprehensive garage-keepers liability insurance while your vehicle is in our custody and during enclosed transport. In no event shall Apex be liable for non-OEM aftermarket paint adhesion failures on vehicles that have undergone prior unbaked body shop repainting.

6. Governing Law

These Terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Arizona and Maricopa County, without regard to its conflict of law provisions.
