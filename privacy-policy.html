Privacy Policy

Apex Paint Protection LLC ("Apex", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, utilize, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or engage our automotive restyling and protection services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you request an estimate, schedule a studio consultation, subscribe to updates, or submit inquiries through our online contact forms. This information may include:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Your full name, email address, telephone number, and physical billing/shipping address.
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle year, make, model, VIN, current paint condition, existing modifications, and service preferences.
  • Log & Device Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, device identifiers, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and navigation patterns automatically collected via server logs and cookies.

2. Cookies, Web Beacons & Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense & Google Ads)

Our website utilizes cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience, analyze website traffic, and deliver personalized commercial advertising:

  • Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to monitor website performance. Google Analytics collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable traffic metrics. You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for legitimate commercial purposes, including:

  • Generating accurate, itemized quotes for paint protection film, ceramic coatings, paint correction, and vinyl wraps.
  • Scheduling studio intake inspections and vehicle service appointments.
  • Responding to customer support questions and providing vehicle aftercare advice.
  • Maintaining website functionality, security, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance.
  • Delivering relevant promotions and studio updates with your explicit consent.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal identifiable information to third parties. We may share data only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist in operating our website, hosting databases, processing payments, or delivering email communications under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Warranty Registration: Submitting vehicle VIN and installation details to authorized film manufacturers (e.g., XPEL, SunTek, STEK) solely to register your 10-year transferable warranty.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or governmental regulatory authority to protect the rights, property, and safety of Apex, our clients, or the public.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area and the State of California), you may have the following legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the specific personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Deletion: Request the erasure of your personal data from our active systems.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at privacy@apexprotectionaz.com or call our studio at (480) 555-0142.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards (including 256-bit SSL encryption) to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.
