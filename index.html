Three ways to protect your paint

Choose one or combine them. Most clients pair PPF on high-impact areas with ceramic coating on everything else.

  • Paint Protection Film

    Self-healing urethane film that absorbs rock chips, road rash, and light scratches. Completely invisible on gloss paint, also available in satin stealth.

    • 8.5 mil film
    • 10-year warranty
    • Self-healing top coat

  • Vinyl Wraps

    Full color changes in gloss, satin, or matte. The factory paint stays protected underneath, and the film removes cleanly when desired.

    • 300+ colors
    • Gloss, satin, matte
    • Fully removable

  • Ceramic Coating

    A hard, hydrophobic layer bonded to paint, wheels, and glass. Deeper gloss, faster washes, and ultimate resistance to desert UV and contaminants.

    • 9H hardness
    • 5-year coating
    • Hydrophobic
