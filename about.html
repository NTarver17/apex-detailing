Our Facility: Clinical Cleanroom Standards

Automotive paint protection film cannot be installed to showroom perfection in an open-air garage or dusty warehouse. Even a microscopic speck of airborne desert dust trapped beneath clear polyurethane creates an unsightly silver bubble that ruins the optic line of a supercar.

At Apex, our Scottsdale installation bays are engineered around a closed-loop climate and air filtration system:

  • Positive Pressure HEPA Air Filtration: Continuous multi-stage air exchange that forces airborne particulate outward, preventing dust intrusion when studio bay doors open.
  • Clinical Hexagonal 5000K Lighting: Over 120,000 lumens of continuous, shadow-free daylight-balanced LED lighting designed to reveal the finest clear coat flaws and edge alignment subtleties.
  • Reverse-Osmosis Deionized Water Systems: Every installation slip solution is mixed using ultra-pure deionized water, eliminating mineral spotting and adhesive fogging.
  • Climate & Humidity Calibration: Ambient temperatures are regulated to a steady 72°F with 45% relative humidity to ensure optimal film elasticity and instant adhesive cure.

Factory Certified Technicians & Craftsmanship Standards

Every master installer at Apex has undergone rigorous hands-on manufacturer certification programs (XPEL Certified Master, SunTek Elite, 3M Preferred, and IDA Skills Validated).

We refuse to rush vehicles through a production assembly line. We dedicate 3 to 5 full days per vehicle for full-body clear bra installations, ensuring every badge is properly recessed, every sensor is custom plotted, and all panel edges are fully wrapped around backside seams.
